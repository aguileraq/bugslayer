# Mutable Widow — Concepto visual v1

## Asset

- `mutable-widow-concept-sheet.png`
- Resolución: 1254 × 1254 px.
- Uso: referencia visual; no es un spritesheet runtime.

## Identidad fijada

- Araña mecánica baja y ancha.
- Ocho patas articuladas.
- Patas delanteras con forma de abrazadera o corchete.
- Dos colmillos-emisores frontales.
- Abdomen octagonal compuesto por tres anillos concéntricos.
- Núcleo energético con forma sutil de reloj de arena.
- Acero negro, azul petróleo y gris.
- Cian para estado estable.
- Verde lima para reasignación.
- Rojo para advertencias y daño.

## Ataques representados

1. Reassignment Volley: descarga lineal doble desde los colmillos.
2. Scope Web: nodos energéticos conectados que preparan varios carriles.
3. Damage: anillos desalineados y descarga controlada.

La hoja también incluye idle energético, colapso, pose inerte, detalle del emisor,
detalle de los anillos, articulación de pata y paleta.

## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production concept sheet for a top-down pixel-art action RPG enemy.
Input image 1 role: layout, camera angle, pixel-art finish, dark technical
concept-sheet presentation, and panel density reference only. Do not copy the
creature, anatomy, orange palette, or specific mechanical parts.

Create an original concept sheet for a single enemy called Mutable Widow, but
include absolutely no written name, labels, letters, numbers, logos, or watermark
in the image.

Subject: a low, wide robotic widow spider representing mutable programming
variables. It must have exactly eight clearly articulated mechanical legs, a
compact armored cephalothorax, two forward fang-shaped linear energy emitters,
and a large octagonal abdomen built from three concentric rotating armor rings
representing nested variable scopes. The abdomen contains a subtle
hourglass-shaped energy core, not a painted biological marking. The two front
legs resemble mechanical clamps or bracket shapes. The silhouette must be
distinctly spider-like, heavy, calculating, and different from the tall
forward-armed mantis in the reference.

Color identity: near-black gunmetal, dark petroleum blue, muted steel gray,
bright cyan stable-energy glow, acid-lime reassignment glow, small controlled red
warning lights, white-hot core highlights. Avoid dominant orange or yellow so it
is immediately distinguishable from Parse Mantis.

Sheet composition: dark desaturated navy technical background divided by thin
muted panel lines. Large three-quarter hero view in the biggest left panel.
Smaller consistent-model views showing front/down, left profile, right profile,
and rear/up. Include three action panels: calm idle with cyan concentric abdomen
pulse; Reassignment Volley with energy traveling from abdomen to both fang
emitters and two dense straight cyan-lime bolts; Scope Web with two or three
small energy nodes forming parallel straight lanes; damage reaction with rings
misaligned and a controlled red-cyan spark. Include a defeated/collapsed pose
with the core dark and all legs folded inward. Bottom close-up panels: fang
emitter mechanism, three nested abdomen rings with hourglass core, one
bracket-shaped leg joint, and seven unlabeled square palette swatches.

Camera and style: classic 16-bit Japanese RPG top-down three-quarter view, about
60 degrees above horizon, crisp hand-placed pixel art, bold dark pixel outline,
limited flat cel-shaded palette, no antialiasing, no gradients, no soft edges, no
texture noise. Consistent identity and proportions in every panel.
Production-quality original robot design, readable at small sprite scale.

Constraints: one and only one character design repeated consistently; exactly
eight legs in every full-body view where anatomy is visible; no humanoid body; no
organic fur; no realistic spider photography; no web background; no
environmental scene; no cast shadows; no text; no labels; no frame numbers; no
watermark; do not resemble or recreate any existing video-game character.
